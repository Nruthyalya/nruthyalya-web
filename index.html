Ashwini Ravi

Bharatanatyam dancer, teacher, Artistic Director of Nruthyalya

Ashwini Ravi is a Bharatanatyam dancer, teacher and choreographer with over two decades of rigorous training and performance experience. She began at the age of four under Guru Smt. Sunanda and continued advanced training for more than 20 years under Guru Prakash S. Iyer.

She completed the Vidwat Examination (Karnataka Secondary Education Examination Board) with distinction and is a Doordarshan B-graded artiste. Her dance is marked by expressive abhinaya, a strong command of nritta, and the ability to carry deep emotion through storytelling.

In 2013 she founded Nruthyalya School of Dance in Bangalore to promote India's traditional art forms. She presents traditional margams and thematic productions rooted in Sanatana Dharma, Bhakti poetry and classical literature. As a teacher she blends technique, sahitya, raga bhava and spiritual depth, so students experience Bharatanatyam as a sadhana, a path of inner growth and cultural continuity.
